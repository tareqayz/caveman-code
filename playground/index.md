# Caveman Code

The world of software engineering is changing. If you're an anti-AI doomer in the computer science field, things are not looking too good right now. AI is getting really good, so knowing how to utilize is properly is more important than ever. One of the big parts of software engineering used to be about the actual coding part, but now it's shifting towards an architect / product manager type of role. Junior developers will need to think big picture, but the issue is that they're expected to figure out how to basically skip over the years of experience being an entry-level junior dev, and start thinking like a senior dev right from the get go.

That being said, I still believe it's important to stay sharp. Use and really on AI too much and you'll become obselete. It's sort of like the dunning kruger effect. 

So here's my solution. Caveman Code. No ChatGPT, no Claude, just raw dogging small coding projects. It may look pathetic, but I'll figure it out as I go. As long as I get a bit better everyday I'll be happy with that.
